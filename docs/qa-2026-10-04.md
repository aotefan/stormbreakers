# AI, water, captain-name and timed supply revision

Local changes only; this revision has not been pushed or deployed.

## Behavior

- AI keeps an engagement distance, turns for broadside fire, leads moving targets, avoids islands and other hulls, and escapes if it makes no progress. Easy/normal/hard change sailing pace, aim, attack interval and repair behavior.
- Safe spawn positions clear islands and other boats. Human takeover stops inherited AI velocity; taking over a sunk AI uses a safe spawn.
- Movement damping uses elapsed simulation time, so a 120 Hz display does not produce different boat handling from a 20 Hz authority.
- Water has displaced waves, moving surf and distorted planar reflections of boats and islands. Low quality uses the cheaper sky-reflection fallback. All materials/textures are generated in code.
- Square sails retain the Tina mark and show the captain name on both sides. Names update when humans replace AI or AI takes over disconnected humans.
- Expired cannonballs and effects release GPU resources. Pausing solo also freezes water and active smoke.

## Automated evidence

`npm test` runs build, AI behavior, authoritative rules, desktop/mobile visual checks, and touch/network prediction checks.

`test-output/ai-revision/behavior.json` includes:

- 20 six-boat spawn checks with no overlap or island intersection.
- A controlled 60-second duel: easy 9 shots, normal 13 shots, hard 20 shots; all levels landed shots and avoided sustained close contact.
- Full fleet behavior at each difficulty; every AI moved, attacked and escaped obstacles.
- Island navigation at 20/60/120 Hz, frame-rate-independent travel, and default 180-second settlement in all three modes.

## Actual browser playthrough

All five scenarios had active AI, damage and cannon fire. Longest living AI idle period was under 0.5 seconds in these runs. Every pause check froze simulation and wave time. No page exceptions were observed. Full detail is in `playthrough.json` and `playthrough.log` under the evidence directory.

`node scripts/render-regression.mjs` separately checks live cannon smoke remains frozen during pause, bounds GPU geometry during repeated fire, and uses two independent browser contexts to verify name synchronization and AI takeover on the existing ship. Textures plateaued at 26; active geometry fluctuated with effects instead of retaining every expired effect. Evidence: `render-regression.json`.

Phone and iPad coverage uses Chromium viewport/touch emulation on this Mac. It is not a claim of physical iOS-device testing. Hardware Chrome uses Apple M1 Pro Metal; default headless Chromium uses SwiftShader and is substantially slower.

## Q/E and timed supply follow-up

- Q fires port/left; E fires starboard/right, relative to the ship's bow. Four headings and both sides are checked against the actual projectile velocity.
- Each normal shot emits one cannonball with 34 damage. Each sea-crate pickup adds one projectile, up to three, and grants a 20% damage bonus (rounded to 41 per projectile) for 15 seconds. Another pickup refreshes the timer. Expiry restores one projectile and base damage; sinking clears the bonus.
- Sea crates expire after 30 seconds and replenish. A shrinking ring indicates remaining time; its final six seconds pulse orange. The HUD shows salvo size, damage bonus and the power timer. Solo pause freezes both timers.
- The instant reload skill is now labeled Reload/装填 to distinguish it from floating supplies.

`scripts/firepower-test.ts` passed direction, real damage (two powered hits reduce 100 HP to 18), count stacking/cap, refresh, expiry, crate replenishment and pause checks. Evidence: `test-output/supply-revision/rules.json`.

`scripts/supply-playthrough.mjs` was run after this follow-up in headed Chrome using two independent browser contexts in the same room, plus two idle WebSocket seats to isolate the timer from AI combat. It uses normal drag and Q/E controls, without changing game state. The player collected a real crate, fired two 41-damage balls from each side, and the other browser observed the same powered state and identical authoritative snapshots. The player then sailed away, waited alive at 100 HP until expiry, and fired one 34-damage ball. No page exceptions occurred. Evidence: `test-output/supply-revision/browser.json`, `browser.log`, `powered-salvo.png`, `power-expired.png`.

The five-mode/difficulty playthrough and render regression described above preceded this follow-up. The final full `npm test` was rerun afterward and passed build, AI, firepower, touch unit, authoritative state, visual and touch visual suites. It rechecked independent-browser state synchronization, hits/HP/score, AI replacement, respawn/settlement, portrait/landscape multi-touch fire and release-stop prediction before server acknowledgement. Full log: `test-output/supply-revision/full-tests.log`.
